Plug your camera into your iPhone with a USB cable. Every frame you shoot appears on your phone at once, and you can drive the shutter and change settings from there.

A camera's rear screen is small, and outdoors it is often unreadable. Tethr puts the frame you just took on the whole of your iPhone display — large enough to judge focus, and large enough to show a client or a subject.

WHAT IT DOES

Shots arrive on their own
Every frame transfers as you take it and opens full-screen. Shooting RAW is no obstacle: Tethr reads the full-resolution preview out of the file itself, so what you see is sharp.

Remote control from your phone
Shutter speed, aperture, ISO, exposure compensation, white balance and shooting mode are all adjustable with a scrubber, always in sync with the camera body. Press the shutter button and the camera focuses first, then fires.

Exposure meter
The same exposure indicator you see in the viewfinder, in the header of the app.

Import to Photos
Pick the frames you want and save them to your iPhone's photo library.

GPS
Tethr uses your iPhone's location to record where each frame was taken. Cameras with no GPS of their own end up with geotagged photographs.

Works with Tethr for Mac
Send your recorded track straight to Tethr running on a Mac on the same network. When you later pull the card and import the RAW files there, the coordinates can be written into all of them at once.

Clock sync
On connection, the camera's internal clock is set from your iPhone.

WHAT YOU NEED

A digital camera that speaks PTP (Picture Transfer Protocol), and a USB cable to connect it to your iPhone. Depending on your devices, a camera adapter may be required. Set the camera's USB mode to MTP/PTP.

ABOUT CAMERA SUPPORT

Tethr is tested with the Nikon D300. Other PTP cameras may work for browsing, previewing and importing. Remote control — the shutter and the exposure settings — depends on vendor-specific commands and may not be available on every model.

PRIVACY

Tethr collects nothing. No analytics, no advertising, no accounts. Your location stays on your device, except when you choose to send it to your own Mac.
