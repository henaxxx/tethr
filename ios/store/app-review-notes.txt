=== APP REVIEW NOTES (paste into App Store Connect → App Review Information → Notes) ===

IMPORTANT — THIS APP REQUIRES EXTERNAL HARDWARE

Tethr connects to a standalone digital camera (DSLR / mirrorless) over a wired USB
connection, using the ImageCaptureCore framework. Without a camera physically
attached, the app will launch and show its "No camera connected" state, and no
further functionality can be exercised.

Because this hardware cannot be supplied to the review team, a demonstration video
of the current build running on a physical iPhone is provided here:

    [DEMO VIDEO URL]

The video shows, in one continuous take:
  1. Launching the app with no camera attached (the disconnected state).
  2. Attaching a Nikon D300 DSLR via a USB camera adapter and the app detecting it.
  3. The connected state: camera model, lens, and current exposure settings read
     live from the camera body.
  4. Changing shutter speed, aperture and ISO from the app, with the camera's own
     top display changing to match.
  5. Releasing the shutter from the app, autofocus running first.
  6. The captured frame arriving in the app and being previewed full-screen.
  7. Importing that frame into the iOS photo library, with GPS coordinates from the
     iPhone written into the file.

NO ACCOUNT IS REQUIRED. There is no sign-in, no server, and no paid content.

PERMISSIONS AND WHY THEY ARE NEEDED

- Location (Always / background):
  The app writes the capture location into photographs as they are imported. A
  photographer typically locks the phone and puts it in a pocket between frames,
  which can be many minutes apart, so a foreground-only authorisation would leave
  gaps in the record and produce photographs with missing or stale coordinates.
  Background location is used solely to keep that positional log continuous.
  Location data never leaves the device except when the user explicitly sends it
  to their own copy of the companion Mac app over the local network.
  The feature is off by default in the sense that no location is read unless the
  user turns logging on in the app.

- Motion & Fitness:
  Used only to tell whether the user is standing still. While they are, GPS is
  switched off and the last precise position is kept, since a photographer
  standing still to shoot has not moved. This is the main battery saving for
  shooting with the phone in a pocket. Motion data is not stored or sent.

- Proximity sensor (no permission prompt):
  With a camera connected, covering the sensor (the phone going into a pocket)
  lets iOS turn the screen off while the app stays in the foreground, so
  capture events and locations keep being recorded. Auto-lock is suspended
  only while the sensor is covered. This can be turned off in the location
  settings sheet.

- Photo library (add only):
  To save imported photographs.

- Local network / Bonjour (_tethr._tcp):
  To discover the user's own Mac running the companion desktop app, so that a
  location log can be handed over for embedding into RAW files stored there. This
  is a direct device-to-device connection; no external server is contacted.

- Camera usage description:
  Required for access to the attached external camera device.

PRIVACY

The app collects nothing. There are no analytics, no advertising identifiers, and
no third-party SDKs of any kind. Its privacy manifest declares no collected data
types and no tracking.
